```html Restock Checklist

📦 Restock Checklist

Enter quantities, then switch to your pick-up checklist.

➕ Add a cigarette

Previously used names will be suggested as you type.

📋 Cigarette quantities

Set the amount you need. Cigarettes with quantities above zero appear in the Pick-up List tab.

Deleting a cigarette keeps its name in your saved suggestions.

🛒 Cigarettes to pick up

Tap “✓ Picked Up” when you have collected the cigarette. It will disappear from this tab, while its quantity stays in the first tab.

🌙 Finish the day

Reset all quantities and the pick-up checklist for your next shift. Your active cigarette names and saved suggestions will remain.
```
