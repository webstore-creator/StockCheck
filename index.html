```html Restock Checklist

📦 Restock Checklist

Enter quantities in the first chart. Your pick-up checklist updates automatically in the second chart.

➕ Add a cigarette

Start typing a name to see names you've used before.

📋 Chart 1 — Enter quantities

Enter how many packs you need for each cigarette. Only quantities above zero appear in the pick-up chart.

Deleting cigarettes removes them from your active list, but their names remain saved as suggestions.

🛒 Chart 2 — Pick-up checklist

When you collect a cigarette from storage, tap “✓ Picked Up”. It will disappear from this chart but remain in Chart 1.

🌙 Finish the day

When your shift is finished, reset the quantities and pick-up checklist for the next day. Your cigarette names will remain saved.
```
